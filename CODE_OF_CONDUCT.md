# Code of conduct

## Our pledge

We want this project to be usable by anyone, whatever their background, identity or experience level. The
hackathon's own code of conduct applies to this repository as well: a harassment-free environment, respectful
collaboration, no discrimination.

Contributors and maintainers pledge to make participation a harassment-free experience for everyone,
regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity
and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

* Be respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences. Quantum cognition is a field with genuine open
  disagreement; disagreement about ideas is welcome, dismissal of people is not.
* Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully. Point to evidence, and say when you are unsure.
* Be careful with claims about human cognition. This library models *responses*, not people; do not use it, or
  contribute to it in a way that stigmatises or diagnoses individuals or groups.
* Respect privacy: no real human subjects data in this repository without documented consent and ethics review.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, insults, discriminatory jokes, sexualised attention, publishing others' private information, and
sustained disruption of discussions.

## Scope and enforcement

This code applies in the repository, in issues and pull requests, and in any space where someone represents the
project. Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainers. Maintainers may remove, edit or reject contributions
that do not follow it, and will explain the reason. Reports are handled confidentially.
