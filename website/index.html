Q-Cog

A Qiskit-native library for modelling, simulating and explaining quantum-probability models of human judgement, decision making and collective behaviour.

What it does

Every model is simulated with Qiskit, reports the same quantity computed by a classical account of the same situation, states the caveats that limit its claims, and draws its circuit wherever one exists. The library checks itself: the self-test table, the figure checks and the documentation checks run in the test suite.
